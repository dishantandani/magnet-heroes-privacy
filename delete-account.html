Delete Your Magnet Heroes Account

You can request deletion of your Magnet Heroes account and associated game data without reinstalling the game.

How to request deletion

Email us using the button below. If your account has a linked email address, please send the request from that address. If you play as a guest, include your in-game hero name and explain that your account is a guest account.

Email: magnetheroes4@gmail.com

Request Account Deletion

Suggested email subject: Delete my Magnet Heroes account

What data will be deleted?

When your deletion request is verified and processed, we intend to delete the account and associated game data, including your hero name, game progress, scores, stars, coins, gems, unlocked heroes, inventory, upgrades, missions, and leaderboard entries.

How long does deletion take?

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. We may need to retain limited information where required by law or reasonably necessary for security or fraud prevention.

Need help?

Contact us at magnetheroes4@gmail.com if you have questions about your deletion request.
