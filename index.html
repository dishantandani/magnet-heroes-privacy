Magnet Heroes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Who we are

Magnet Heroes ("the game", "we", "us") is a mobile platform game. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. By playing, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Hero name: the nickname you choose. It is shown to other players on the leaderboards.

Game progress: levels cleared, stars, best scores, coins, gems, heroes unlocked, items in your bag, missions, daily gift streaks and upgrades.

Account identifier: when you start playing we create an anonymous guest ID so your progress can be saved. It is not linked to your real identity.

Email address and password (optional): only if you choose "Save progress" to keep your progress across devices. Passwords are stored encrypted and we never see them.

On your device only: sound, vibration and control settings are stored on your device and are never sent to us.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, phone number or advertising ID. The game does not show ads and does not use third-party tracking or analytics. There are no real-money purchases; coins and gems are earned only by playing.

How we use information

To save your progress and restore it when you return, to show leaderboards, to deliver daily gifts and missions, and to keep the game fair (for example, checking that rewards are earned). We never sell, rent or share your information for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Game data is stored securely with our cloud database provider, Supabase, which processes it only on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and access is restricted so each player can only read and change their own records.

What other players can see

Only your hero name, scores and leaderboard position. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your hero name, progress, coins, gems, heroes, items and leaderboard entries are permanently erased right away. We do not keep backups of deleted accounts beyond our provider's standard short-term recovery window (up to 7 days), after which they are gone.

Delete your account and data

In the game: open Settings, scroll to "Privacy & account" and tap "Delete account". This removes everything immediately.

Without the app: email magnetheroes4@gmail.com from the address linked to your account (or include your hero name) with the subject "Delete my Magnet Heroes account". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 beyond a nickname and game progress. Adding an email is optional; if you are under 13, please ask a parent before doing so. Parents who believe their child has given us personal information can contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to how it is used. You can rename your hero and delete your account in Settings, or contact us for anything else.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date below and, for important changes, let you know in the game.

Contact
